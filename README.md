# BookRecs

A community-driven book review and recommendation platform where readers can share reviews, rate their favorite reads, and discover their next favorite book through genre-based search.

---

## Features

* **Book Reviews & Ratings:** Share your detailed thoughts and rate books on a 5-star scale to guide fellow readers.
* **Genre-Based Discovery:** Browse and search books filtered by specific genres, tropes, and categories.
* **Community Recommendations:** Discover crowd-sourced book recommendations tailored to your reading interests.
* **Personalized Reading Lists:** Keep track of books you have read, are currently reading, or plan to read.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3
* **Backend:** Python (Flask)
* **Database:** SQLite / PostgreSQL
